What Hourfolk provides

Hourfolk provides world-clock, time-zone planning, and local reminder tools. The extension is provided for personal and professional planning convenience. No account or payment is required.

Reminder limitations

Hourfolk reminders rely on Chrome’s alarms and notifications. Chrome must be running for an alert to be delivered. If your device is asleep or Chrome is closed at the scheduled time, an alert may arrive late or after Chrome restarts. Delivery time can also be affected by browser, operating-system, notification, battery, and device settings.

Do not rely on Hourfolk for emergencies, safety-critical events, medical treatment, financial deadlines, or any situation where a missed or delayed alert could cause harm or loss.
